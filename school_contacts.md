# IU Bloomington Graduate Contact Directory

Contacts for graduate admissions and program questions at specific IU
Bloomington schools and departments. Contact the individual school or
department for program-specific admissions requirements, deadlines, and
application status. The IU Graduate School Bloomington handles general
graduate questions only.

Source: IU Bloomington Graduate Contact Directory, prepared August 2026.
